**Built:** branch `sprint/ask-your-library` → PR #7, merged as `84d421f`. 17 files, +680/−57.

`POST /api/ask {q}` → `{question, answer, sources, model, reason, error}`.

- `rapport/ask.py` — the whole feature above the model call, and all of it pure enough to test on Linux:
  `keywords()` strips punctuation and English stopwords (and keeps them when a question is nothing else);
  `db.search(match="any")` because a question is not a phrase anyone ever said, so requiring every term finds
  nothing; `pick_hits()` caps at two hits per recording and eight in all; `passages()` grows each hit into the turn
  before and after (`db.segment_context`) and drops hits another excerpt already covers; `build_user()` numbers
  them; `cited()` reads the `[n]` back out of the answer so the UI can dim the excerpts the answer did not use.
- **No model is a state, not an error.** With none installed the excerpts come back on their own under "moments
  that match", and `reason` says `no_model`. Same for `no_matches`, `model_error` and `empty_answer` — the moments
  survive all four. Nothing is invented to cover a missing model, which is the whole point of doing this locally.
- `summarize.chat(provider, model, system, user)` is new: the one-turn model call summaries and Ask now share.
  `_ollama_chat` took a `timeout` and `_mlx_chat` a `max_tokens`; Ask asks for 600 tokens and 180 s, a summary is
  unchanged at 900 and 600 s.
- UI: Search and Ask are two tabs on the Search page, both `keepMounted` so an answer survives a trip to Search.
  Citations are buttons that jump to their excerpt; an excerpt opens the recording a second before the words.
  The Markdown renderer moved out of `summary-tab.tsx` into `components/markdown.tsx` (with an optional `cite`
  hook) and the snippet renderer out of `search-view.tsx` into `components/snippet.tsx`; both were about to be
  duplicated a second time.
- Docs: a new section 7 in `docs/how-it-works.md`, the route and the module in `docs/developers.md`, a README
  bullet, `CHANGELOG.md` under Unreleased, and `docs/roadmap.md` corrected — it still had Ask under "Later" and
  summary templates under "Next" a day after they shipped.
