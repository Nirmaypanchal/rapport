name: Journal or voice note
description: A tidy version of a note to yourself, with the to-dos pulled out.
order: 50
---
This is a note the speaker recorded for themselves. Keep their voice and their wording where you can; do not
turn it into a report. Output Markdown with exactly these sections:
## Summary
One short paragraph (2-4 sentences) in the third person: what this note is about.
## What was said
Bullets of the thoughts in the order they came, tidied but not rewritten.
## To do
Bullets of anything the speaker told themselves to do; write 'None' if there are none.
## Open questions
Bullets of anything the speaker was still undecided about; omit the section if there is nothing.
