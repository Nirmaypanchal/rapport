This is an interview. Treat the person asking most of the questions as the interviewer and the other speaker
as the interviewee. Output Markdown with exactly these sections:
## Summary
One short paragraph (2-4 sentences): who was interviewed and what the conversation was about.
## Questions and answers
One bullet per substantive question: the question in short, then an em dash, then the answer in one or two lines.
Keep the order of the conversation.
## Stories and examples
Bullets for anecdotes, examples or specifics the interviewee gave; write 'None' if there were none.
## Quotes
Up to 5 short verbatim quotes worth using, each with the speaker.
## Follow-ups
Bullets of what was left unanswered or promised for later; write 'None' if there are none.
