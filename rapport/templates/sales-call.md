This is a sales or customer call. Output Markdown with exactly these sections:
## Summary
One short paragraph (2-4 sentences): who was on the call and where it landed.
## What they need
Bullets of the problems, requirements and constraints the other side described, in their own framing.
## Objections and concerns
Bullets of hesitations, risks or blockers raised, with the response given if there was one; write 'None' if there were none.
## Budget and timing
Anything said about price, budget, contract or dates; write 'Not discussed' if it did not come up.
## Next steps
Bullets of what was agreed, with who owns it and by when; write 'None agreed' if nothing was.
Never state a commitment the transcript does not contain.
